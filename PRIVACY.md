# Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026.

## The calculator

ArcheAge Chronicles Crafting Calculator does not collect personal information, usage analytics, or crash reports. It does not require an account. Crafting and trading selections, quantities, and calculations stay on your device. **Copy plan** puts the plan on your Windows clipboard when you choose it.

The app stores your named plans, craft lists, inventory counts, gathered materials, and completed steps locally in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ArcheAgeChroniclesCraftingData\plans.json`. It uses that file to reopen your plans after a restart or update.

## Updates and external links

An installed copy checks GitHub Releases at startup and downloads an update when one is available. GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address. These requests do not include your craft list. [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

Material source links open in your browser when you activate them. If there is no direct source link for a material, the app opens a Google search containing the material name. The sites you visit handle those requests under their own privacy policies. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

The Trade Helper calculates routes using the bundled catalog. RaidDex links open in your browser when you activate them.
