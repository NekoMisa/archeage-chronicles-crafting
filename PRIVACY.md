# Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026.

## The calculator

ArcheAge Chronicles Crafting Calculator does not collect personal information, usage analytics, or crash reports. It does not require an account. Crafting selections, quantities, and calculations stay on your device. **Copy plan** puts the plan on your Windows clipboard when you choose it.

Before installing an update, the app temporarily saves your craft list on your device so it can restore the list after restarting. The app removes the temporary copy after restoring it.

## Updates and external links

An installed copy checks GitHub Releases at startup and downloads an update when one is available. GitHub receives standard request information such as your IP address. These requests do not include your craft list. [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

Material source links open in your browser when you activate them. If there is no direct source link for a material, the app opens a Google search containing the material name. The sites you visit handle those requests under their own privacy policies. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
